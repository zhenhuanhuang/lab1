Designing clear and engaging digital experiences

Hello, I am Zhenhuan Huang, a fourth-year student at the University of Toronto Mississauga, majoring in Communication, Culture, Information & Technology and Economics. My work explores how web development, interactive media, visual communication, and economic thinking can come together to create useful and engaging experiences. The projects below represent three areas of my academic and creative practice.

Responsive Portfolio Website

A five-page personal portfolio created to present creative work, professional experience, and personal identity in a clear, responsive format. The site combines structured navigation, project cards, modal previews, progress indicators, and layouts adapted for different screen sizes.
